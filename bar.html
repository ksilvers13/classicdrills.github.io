Cost and managerial

  • Breakeven units = fixed costs ÷ contribution margin per unit. Add target profit to fixed costs for target units.
  • Materials price variance = (AP − SP) × AQ. Quantity variance = (AQ − SQ) × SP. Positive is unfavorable.
  • High-low: variable rate = change in cost ÷ change in activity, using the highest and lowest activity levels.
  • Only avoidable, future costs that differ between choices are relevant.

Financial analysis

  • Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities. Quick ratio = (cash + marketable securities + receivables) ÷ current liabilities.
  • Inventory turnover = COGS ÷ average inventory. ROE = net income ÷ average equity.

Technical accounting

  • Goodwill = consideration + fair value of NCI − fair value of identifiable net assets.
  • Equity method: investment + share of income − share of dividends.

Government

  • Governmental funds: modified accrual, revenue when measurable and available (property taxes: within about 60 days).
  • Government-wide, proprietary and fiduciary: full accrual.
