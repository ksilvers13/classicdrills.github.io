Audit risk model

  • Audit risk = Risk of material misstatement × Detection risk. RMM = Inherent risk × Control risk.
  • Acceptable detection risk = Audit risk ÷ (IR × CR). Higher RMM means lower acceptable detection risk, so more substantive work: more effective procedures, done closer to year-end, with larger samples.
  • The auditor assesses IR and CR but controls only detection risk.

Vouching vs. tracing

  • Vouch: start from the recorded entry, go back to the source document. Tests existence or occurrence (overstatement).
  • Trace: start from the source document, go forward to the records. Tests completeness (understatement).
