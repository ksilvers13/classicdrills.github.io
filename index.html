Accounting Drill Pads

Ten practice drills, from your first journal entries to every section of the CPA exam. Every question is generated fresh and graded instantly with the work shown. Questions you miss are saved so you can redo them until you get them right.

Fundamentals

  1. Debit Drill Journal entries and debit/credit rules for everyday transactions.
  2. Period-End Drill Adjusting entries and how the three financial statements tie together.
  3. Formula Drill Real Excel formulas, graded by a built-in formula engine, from SUM to XLOOKUP and NPV.
  4. Tax Return Basics Drill Filing status, the standard deduction and the Form 1040 flow, with tax year 2026 figures.

CPA Exam: Core Sections

Every CPA candidate takes all three.

  1. AUD Drill Auditing and attestation: risk, evidence, internal control, sampling and audit reports.
  2. FAR Drill Financial Accounting and Reporting: current liabilities, bonds, leases, stockholders' equity, EPS, cash flows, revenue recognition and income taxes.
  3. REG Drill Regulation: individual tax, property transactions, business law and tax ethics.

CPA Exam: Discipline Sections (pick one)

Each candidate chooses one discipline to take alongside the core.

  1. BAR Drill Business Analysis and Reporting: managerial accounting, ratios, business combinations and government.
  2. ISC Drill Information Systems and Controls: IT controls, SOC reports, security and continuity.
  3. TCP Drill Tax Compliance and Planning: entities, partnerships and S corps, gifts and estates, planning.
